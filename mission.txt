Mission statement 
RICT’s mission is to understand what our students and businesses hope to achieve and dedicate our computer training and development to the fulfillment of those goals.

The computer training provided is contributing visibly and substantially to the fulfillment of our area business's strategies.

RICT will maintain financial balance, charge a value for our services, and deliver an even higher value to our clients. RICT will make computer user techniques accessible to hundreds of computer users who would otherwise not have the updated knowledge to use them. RICT will make a profit and generate cash.

The Academy will provide a rewarding work environment and fair compensation to our employees; ultimately provide excellent value to our customers and a fair return to our owners.
